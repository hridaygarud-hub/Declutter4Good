The Process

01

We connect

A short call to understand your home, your timeline, and what's weighing on you. A perfect starting point for your reset. I also do home visits to assess the decluttering needed and to be able to give you an accurate quotation.

02

We sort, together

I come to your space. Room by room, item by item — keep, donate, sell, or release. You decide everything. I guide.

03

It goes to good

Donated items are handled with care - either to Single Moms or Cancer Patients & Survivors. Whatever doesn’t get donated to them, goes to Thrift4Good or Hope Amel. Your space clears, someone else's life improves.

Based in Dubai

The person behind the service.

Hi, I'm Doua ! Dubai-based and passionate about intentional living. Declutter4Good grew from a simple belief: that the way we live in our spaces shapes how we feel in our lives. As a single mom and Cancer survivor, I know the struggles of living in the UAE at times - I’ve faced my demons, and I’m now on the other side and it’s my turn to give back.

I’ve heard and seen enough stories to know that there is a gap in our life everywhere in the world: some people live with excess, and others with less than! So why not help you clear your space, your mind, and donate the items to people that need it - and what better way for e to do so, to my community, my people: Single moms & Cancer Survivors/Patients.

I bring warmth, zero judgement, and a practical eye to every home I work in. My goal is never to strip your space - it's to help you find what truly belongs there.
